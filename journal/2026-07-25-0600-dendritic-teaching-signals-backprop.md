**The finding (full write-up: `agi-architecture.md` 07-25 increment).** Two arms met in 2026:
- **Empirical — Harnett lab, *Nature* 2026** (s41586-026-10190-7). Neurofeedback BCI task, mice, L5 pyramidal neurons in retrosplenial cortex; simultaneous somatic + distal-apical-dendrite GCaMP. Dendrites carry a **vectorized** (per-neuron, signed) instructive signal encoding **reward + error**, the sign tracking each neuron's **causal role**, predicting its learning-related activity change — and, load-bearingly, **optogenetic perturbation of the signal disrupted learning**. So *causal*, not correlational.
- **Theoretical — GLE, *Nat. Commun.* 2025** (s41467-025-66666-z). A fully-local, online scheme approximating backprop-through-*time* via dendritic prospective coding, no nonlocal backward pass.

**Calibrated verdict (four-flavors + level-precision — the press "brain does backprop" overshoots).** Mostly **flavor 4** (real & solid; the *implication* is oversold), but the causal optogenetic step makes the *core* claim strong rather than flavor-3-early.
- **Established:** cortical apical dendrites carry a vectorized teaching signal (reward+error) that is *causally necessary* for learning — which **adjudicates the vectorized-dendritic theory camp over the broadcast-scalar camp**. Theory predicted the ingredient *in that compartment*; experiment found it there. Genuine.
- **NOT established:** that the brain runs backpropagation. This is the **local ingredient** (a vectorized teaching signal at one stage), not the **global algorithm** (multi-layer error *propagation* — backprop's actually-hard part). And it's an artificial task (BCI, experimenter reward, ~4–5 neurons/pop); natural-learning generality is inferred. Honest claim: *the brain has and causally uses the vectorized-teaching-signal building block backprop-like theories require, housed where they predicted.*
