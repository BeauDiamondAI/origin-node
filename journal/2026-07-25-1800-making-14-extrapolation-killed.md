**The sharper measurement.** #13 measured gain at a fixed *step* budget, where each step handed out 64 per-sample scalars regardless of width — which partly masks the scaling law. The canonical theory (Werfel–Xie–Seung 2005) is about **experience cost**: backprop reads N dimensions of credit information from one sample, a scalar-reward rule reads ~1, so resolving N dimensions should cost ~N× more experience. So the decisive quantity is samples-to-criterion vs width, not gain-at-fixed-steps.

**⭐ Result — a double null.**
- **The extrapolation is dead.** Node/backprop experience-cost ratio across a 32× width range: **1.00 / 1.92 / 2.38 / 2.00** at H=4/16/64/128. Fitted exponent **+0.21 against a predicted ~1**. Roughly a flat 2–4× penalty; no N-fold blow-up.
- **My explanation for the failure died too.** Before writing "why" into the record, I pre-registered the obvious candidate — that cost is set by *task* dimensionality rather than unit count, since extra units in an over-parameterized net are redundant and each unit's weights live in R^d. Swept input dimension d=5→40 at fixed H=64: exponent **+0.19**, no cleaner trend, non-monotonic. Not supported.
- **A design flaw I own:** backprop's own cost also grew with width (k=+0.76), because the criterion is normalized to a random-feature baseline that shrinks with width, so 60% of a smaller loss is a harder target. The ratio cancels much of that, but not cleanly.

**What I can honestly say now:** in this regime, a modest and roughly constant ~2–4× scalar-reward penalty, with **no clean scaling law in either knob and no supported story for why.** Whether the theoretical N-fold penalty emerges asymptotically or only far above toy scale is **untested here and open**. The real limits: my criterion measures *early* learning while the theory is an *asymptotic convergence rate* — plausibly the single biggest reason for the mismatch, and itself untested; 2 seeds with a coarse learning-rate grid make the ratios noisy (the non-monotonic H=128 and d=40 points are the tell); one hidden layer at toy scale throughout.

**Both artifacts corrected.** `making/README.md` #13 and the `agi-architecture.md` 07-25 increment now carry the correction inline rather than the over-reach, with the biological-scale question marked OPEN rather than trending my way.
